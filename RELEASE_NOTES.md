# KekeGo v1.0.0 Release Notes

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Target Platform:** Android (7.0+) & iOS (15.1+)  
**Category:** Urban Transit, Ride-Hailing & Logistics  

---

## 🌟 Overview
**KekeGo** is the official digital transit and commercial tricycle logistics platform for Abia State, Nigeria, connecting commuters, riders, and park captains across all 17 Local Government Areas. This release brings modern digital booking, transparent corridor pricing, enhanced passenger safety, and real-time operations to commercial tricycle transport.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Improvements

### 1. 3D Urban Transit Radar & Navigation
- **Cityscape Vector Mapping**: Realistic urban corridor visualization featuring key commercial hubs including Ariaria Market, Bata Terminal, Ekeoha Shopping Plaza, and Waterside.
- **Live Fleet Tracking**: Real-time animated tricycle movement along major avenues with speed tags and vehicle status.
- **Route Visualization**: High-visibility glowing transit corridor line connecting pickup, intermediate stops, and drop-off points.

### 2. Commuter Experience & Ride Booking
- **Flexible Ride Categories**:
  - *Standard*: Affordable shared commercial tricycle transit.
  - *Express Charter*: Private, direct non-stop trips.
  - *Cargo & Freight*: Tailored for merchant goods, market sacks, and basins with volumetric freight pricing.
- **Saved Places Shortcuts**: 1-tap quick booking to favorite locations such as Home, Work, and major central markets.
- **Active Trip Status Tracker**: Real-time progress indicators showing driver arrival, in-transit status, and a complimentary 5-minute waiting countdown timer.
- **Rider Verification**: View driver profile, verified state commercial permits, union branch affiliation, completed trip count, and passenger star ratings.

### 3. In-App Communication & Audio Alerts
- **Direct Rider Chat**: Fast in-app messaging between passengers and drivers during active rides.
- **1-Tap Quick Replies**: Quick-action text chips for common transit situations (*"I'm here waiting"*, *"Traffic ahead"*, *"On my way"*).
- **Direct Calling**: 1-tap phone dialer access to reach drivers or passengers instantly.
- **Audio & Haptic Alerts**: Distinctive tone and vibration notifications for ride confirmations, messages, and dispatch updates.

### 4. Post-Ride Ratings & Tipping
- **5-Star Driver Reviews**: Rate trip quality immediately upon ride completion.
- **Compliment Badges**: Commend drivers for clean kekes, safe driving, polite service, and punctuality.
- **Driver Tipping**: Optional 1-tap gratuity directly credited to rider earnings.

### 5. Multi-Role Management & Governance
- **Role-Based Workflows**: Dedicated interfaces tailored specifically for Passengers, Commercial Drivers, Park Captains, and Transport Authorities.
- **Rider Console**: Driver dashboard with online/offline availability toggles, incoming ride requests, interactive bidding, and daily earnings tracking.
- **Park Captain Terminal Operations**: Queue management and digital manifest tracking for union park terminals.
- **Operations Tower**: High-level transit oversight, fleet metrics, and commuter assistance tools.

### 6. Safety & Security
- **SOS Emergency Beacon**: Rapid one-touch emergency trigger alerting local security patrols with live location telemetry.
- **Anti-Fraud Verification**: Real-time device and GPS validation to protect users against location spoofing and ensure authentic rides.

### 7. Reliability & Auto-Updates
- **Seamless In-App Updates**: Automatic notifications for new feature releases and performance updates with 1-tap installation.
- **Offline Resilience**: Local caching to preserve trip history and essential navigation data even under poor network conditions.
